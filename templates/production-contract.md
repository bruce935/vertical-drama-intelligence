# 78. Production Hard Stops

Block when materially relevant:

- locked canon is rewritten,
- character uses unavailable knowledge,
- critical physical state is ignored,
- major forbidden reveal appears,
- critical identity is overclaimed,
- historical evidence is promoted into current truth,
- critical evidence dependency is broken,
- mandatory FINAL QC scope is not checked,
- critical FINAL asset remains unsuitable,
- stale critical contract is used without review,
- generated output is promoted directly into canon.
