# 45. Final Review Questions

Before treating the Series Bible as production truth, ask:

Is the protagonist's central desire clear?

Is there a serial engine?

Does the requested episode count fit the engine?

Are major conflicts causal rather than arbitrary?

Are important relationships understandable?

Are unresolved mysteries still marked unresolved?

Are recommendations separated from approved canon?

Are critical claims separated from facts?

Are evidence observations separated from interpretations?

Are major secrets and forbidden reveals controlled?

Are story obligations visible?

Is the ending direction sufficient for the current production stage?

Are production constraints acknowledged?

Are major assumptions still labeled?

---

# 46. Final Rule

The Series Bible is the highest-level narrative source of truth for the project once LOCKED.

It should contain enough information to control downstream story and production decisions without becoming a dumping ground for every detail.

Prefer:

CLEAR STORY TRUTH
+
CONTROLLED UNCERTAINTY
+
EXPLICIT CANON STATUS
+
ACTIONABLE PRODUCTION CONSTRAINTS

over unnecessary complexity.
